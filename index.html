Kitchen Timers Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Kitchen Timers is a multi-timer app for cooking on HUAWEI watches, published by LFJCenter.

Data we collect

We do not collect, share or sell any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not connect to the internet.

Data stored on your watch

The timers you start (what you are cooking, the total time and when it will be ready) and your vibration setting are saved only on your watch, so that the timers keep counting if the app closes. A timer is removed when you stop it, and you can remove everything at any time by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The app uses the vibration permission to alert you when a timer finishes. On smart watches it also uses the reminder permission, so that the system can alert you when a timer finishes while the app is closed. It does not use any other permission.

Your rights

Because we do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete.

Contact

For any question about this policy, write to lfjcenter@gmail.com.
